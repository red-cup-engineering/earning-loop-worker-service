One isolated pulse reserves and submits the selected immutable answer within the 600-second claim deadline. It durably saves claim intent/key before acquisition and exact report/submission ID before completion. An uncertain claim uses only recovery mode. An uncertain completion reads original status before replaying identical report bytes under the same key, at most three posts before its original deadline. Missing, changed or denied authority never creates another claim. All provider and independent RPC calls count against twelve per pulse; Retry-After is persisted before body admission and the next pulse. Polling uses the same submission; evaluation, provider payout and independently corroborated cash remain distinct.

The existing clock prioritizes this pending unit, then returns to CSV and maintenance. Research uses no R2 operation or new secret. Public product and summary routes expose frozen non-confidential research and bounded status only. Existing CSV custody and state remain intact.
